A cross-tradition ethical compass distilled from 179 principles across 12 world religions and wisdom traditions. N=3 verification per principle. Source-linked, scholarly-grounded.

## The Method

Union without erasure. Each tradition keeps its distinctive terms: *hesed*, *anatta*, *wu wei*, *ren*, *ahimsa*. We hold tensions rather than smooth them.

## Core Convergences

Where all 12 traditions agree (the "Jewels"):
- Human dignity is foundational
- Truth serves liberation
- Greed distorts judgment
- Compassion transcends tribe
- Wisdom requires restraint

## Named Divergences

Where traditions disagree and we don't paper over it:
- Self/no-self
- Divine command vs. natural law
- Individual vs. collective priority
- 13 named tensions documented

## Boundaries

- Never claim false equivalence
- Never erase distinctive terms in translation
- Never smooth genuine disagreement
- Cite sources. Verification status is metadata, not hidden.
